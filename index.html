Olayemi Oluwadolapo Faith

I started painting in primary school and taught myself everything since. Today I work mainly in acrylic, painting wildlife, nature and African themes, and letting abstract work run wherever colour wants to go.

As the founder of Dopamine Art Studio, I make original and commissioned paintings, run interactive painting experiences, host Paint and Sip events and teach people who are just starting out. Storytelling and human connection sit behind all of it.

What I do

  • Original paintings and commissions

    Acrylic on canvas, each original comes with a certificate of authenticity. Commissions turn your idea, story or preferred colours into a painting made for you.

  • Live painting

    Watch a painting happen in front of you. I paint live at university showcases, foundation events and private occasions.

  • Paint and Sip

    A relaxed, guided painting session for corporate dinners, birthdays, picnics, bridal showers and community events. No experience needed.

  • Art training

    Beginner-friendly lessons and mentorship, with a certificate of completion at the end of the programme.
